About

Hi, I'm Nikunj — a Robotics & Embedded Electronics Engineer at Texas Robotics, UT Austin. I hold an M.S. in Robotics Engineering from Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI) and a B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering from Nirma University (NU), India, where I was also a researcher at the Idea Lab.

My interests lie in intelligent robotics, autonomous vehicle technology, computer vision, and controls — and how they come together to make machines a little more capable. I got hooked in my freshman year watching robots take on real problems, from agriculture to space science, and the fact that they're still far from perfect is what keeps me working on them.
