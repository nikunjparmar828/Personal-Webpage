About

Hi, I'm Nikunj — a Robotics Software Engineer who builds autonomy that works outside the lab. I'm currently a Robotics & Embedded Electronics Engineer at Texas Robotics, working on autonomous mobile robots and open-source ROS2 tools.

I'm most interested in autonomous navigation and motion planning, robot perception, and learning-based autonomy — especially bringing modern AI, including large language models, into robots that are reliable, scalable, and maintainable in the real world. I enjoy working across the full stack, from simulation to on-robot debugging to long-term deployment.

I hold an M.S. in Robotics Engineering from Worcester Polytechnic Institute and a B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering from Nirma University.
